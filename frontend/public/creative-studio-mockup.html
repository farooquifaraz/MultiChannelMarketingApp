3 · Save & launch

Each locked channel is saved as its own template. Email & WhatsApp go straight into a campaign draft → delivery.

Locked content→Channel template→Campaign draft→Contact group→Send / schedule

💾 Save to Templates

Locked channels → Email, WhatsApp, Instagram & Facebook templates (auto-tagged by channel + shared groups kept together).

✉️ Email campaign

Draft from the Email template → choose group → send or schedule.

💬 WhatsApp campaign

Draft from the WhatsApp template → choose group → send or schedule.
